# Cockpit audit after implementation

Scope: the new autopilot panel and its navigation. No actionable findings.
Existing office visuals outside that surface were not redesigned.

Direction: existing terminal instrument frame for the founder's local worker.
ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1. State is the focal point; whitespace separates
status from events. The active tab supplies the established accent. Monospace
is native terminal typography; no font, visual asset or illustration was added.
