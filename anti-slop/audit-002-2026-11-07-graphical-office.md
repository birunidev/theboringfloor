# Graphical Office audit after implementation

Scope: `cmd/officeview`, using the founder's VISUAL_SPEC.md. No actionable
visual findings. Optional telemetry limitations are disclosed in VERIFICATION.md.

Design read: a local monitoring office for the founder, using the supplied
Birunidev white/black/cobalt system and flat geometric SVG. ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 /
MOTION 1. Confirmed transitions have short finite feedback; idle has none.
The current card/stage is the focal point; office stations explain the process.
Whitespace separates status, process, outcomes and activity. Cobalt marks actual
work/focus. The supplied desk/character geometry is the identity motif.

Major decisions come directly from the contract: normative semantic tokens for
state contrast, system fonts for offline delivery, mono IDs/counts for scanning,
the coordinate grid for desk placement, compact process-order stations on phone,
and source-backed badges instead of guessed progress. Semantic success/warning/
danger colors carry real outcomes/control/budget facts rather than decoration.
